Help at the moment a first SIP is about to lapse

An in-app companion that shows up when an instalment fails, when someone taps "Stop", or when the portfolio turns red. It explains what happened in plain words and puts the reversible options next to the final one. It never says which fund to buy or whether to stay invested.

Try a scenario

For user testing

  1. Scenario 1: find out why your November instalment failed and sort it out.
  2. Scenario 2: you're short this month. Do what you'd really do.
  3. Scenario 3: work out whether you've lost money.

Taps are logged anonymously (scenario, button, time) so tests produce numbers. Anything you type goes only to the matching engine and is never stored.

Why these three moments

Loading what the discovery engine found…

A survey of 21 investors describing their first SIP pointed the same way for scenario 3: 4 of the 6 who check weekly or more had thought about stopping, against 4 of the 15 who check monthly or less. Investors who stayed past year one mostly look at what they own, or don't look at all.

How it decides

  • Rules first. A failed instalment shows the bank's reason code in plain words. No AI is needed for facts the app already has.
  • AI only for free text. When someone explains in their own words, the discovery engine matches it to a pattern. Four checks must pass (on-topic, close match, real SIP story, agreement) before it picks a card.
  • Safe fallback. If any check fails, it shows every option instead of guessing. You can open "How we picked this" on any answer.
  • Measured. On 22 hand-labelled posts it had never seen, it confidently showed a specific help card 9 times and 7 were the right one. The 2 misses sent a fund question to the numbers explainer and a stock-trading complaint to payment help. The other 13 posts got "which is closer?" or the full menu. None of the cards contain advice, so a wrong card costs a tap, not a bad decision.
