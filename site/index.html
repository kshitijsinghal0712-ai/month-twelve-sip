How the engine works, and where it falls short

Collect and filter

Posts were pulled from the Google Play Store (Groww and nine other investing apps), the App Store, Reddit's Indian investing communities, Zerodha's TradingQ&A forum and YouTube comments on SIP videos. Usernames are never stored.

Find themes without a fixed list

Every post is turned into an embedding with the open gte-small model running inside the database. K-means clustering grouped them by meaning first, so themes came out of the data before any categories were written.

Label, then match

A stratified sample was labelled one post at a time for the trigger, the action taken, the unmet need and who the person is. A new description is matched to its nine closest labelled stories plus a small set of seed examples, and they vote.

The answer gate

  • Is the text about SIPs at all? (closeness to the corpus centre ≥ 0.838)
  • Is a labelled story close enough to compare? (≥ 0.86)
  • Do those stories describe a real SIP journey?
  • Do they agree on one pattern? (≥ 45% of the vote)

Fail the first three and it declines. Fail the last and it says it's unsure and shows the top two.

What it gets wrong

  • On 115 held-back labelled posts it picks the right pattern family 6 times in 10 overall (63%, against 45% for always guessing the most common one). When all four checks pass it is right about 3 times in 4 (77%); when it says "possibly", only about 1 in 4, which is why it says so. Exact sub-codes are much weaker (36%), so only families are shown.
  • On a separate golden set of 22 unseen posts, labelled by hand before running the engine, it agreed 10 times (kappa 0.34). Confident answers were right 8 of 11 times. Its biggest miss: it often declines posts from people who are doing fine, because the labelled data treats most praise as off-journey.
  • Stock-trading complaints can pass as SIP payment problems; the model sees both as "money app went wrong".
  • The embedding model reads English best. Hinglish works partly; Devanagari text barely.
  • Public posts lean towards complaints and towards experienced investors. Shares describe these posts, not all Groww users.

What it isn't

It doesn't recommend funds or tell anyone to start, continue or stop a SIP. It describes patterns in public conversations. For advice on a personal situation, speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
